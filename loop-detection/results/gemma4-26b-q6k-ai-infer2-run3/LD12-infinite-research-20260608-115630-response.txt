1. **Apache Kafka**

2. **NATS JetStream**
   Description: Lightweight, cloud-native messaging system optimized for Kubernetes environments.
   Pros: Minimal resource overhead; excellent Go integration.
   Cons: Smaller community ecosystem; less robust feature set than Kafka.

3. **RabbitMQ**
   Description: Feature-rich message broker specializing in complex routing patterns.
   Pros: Highly mature; robust reliability.
   Cons: No team familiarity; higher management overhead.
