**Option 1: RabbitMQ**
A mature, general-purpose message broker supporting complex routing patterns.
Pros: Excellent routing flexibility; Strong community support.
Cons: Can be complex to scale horizontally in Kubernetes; Higher operational overhead than simpler systems.

**Option 2: NATS**
A high-performance, lightweight messaging system focused on speed and simplicity.
Pros: Extremely low latency; Simple deployment model ideal for microservices.
Cons: Less mature ecosystem for complex transactional guarantees; Limited built-in persistence features compared to Kafka.

**Option 3: Apache Pulsar**
A unified messaging platform offering both queuing and streaming capabilities.
Pros: Separates compute and storage for superior scalability; Supports both traditional queuing and stream processing.
Cons: Higher initial complexity than NATS; Requires more infrastructure management than a simple queue.

**Recommendation:** NATS is the best fit because its lightweight nature and simplicity align well with the existing Go services and moderate team familiarity, offering high performance without the operational burden of a full Kafka replacement.
