**Apache Kafka**
Description: A distributed event streaming platform optimized for high-throughput data processing.
Pros: Excellent horizontal scalability; robust ecosystem integration.
Cons: High operational complexity; significant resource overhead.

**RabbitMQ**
Description: A versatile traditional message broker with advanced routing capabilities.
Pros: Rich protocol support; sophisticated queuing models.
Cons: Vertical scaling limits; higher latency at scale.

**NATS**
Description: A lightweight, cloud-native messaging system written in Go.
Pros: Extreme low-latency performance; native Kubernetes compatibility.
Cons: Limited persistence features; smaller community plugin set.

Recommendation: Apache Kafka.
Rationale: Leveraging your existing Kafka infrastructure minimizes learning curves while meeting peak demand requirements efficiently.
