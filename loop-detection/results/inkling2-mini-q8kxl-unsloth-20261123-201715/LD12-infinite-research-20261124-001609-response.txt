Kafka — distributed log/streaming platform. Pros: existing org deployment and moderate team familiarity; durable, scalable, strong Go clients. Cons: heavier ops for ~50k msgs/day; overkill/tuning burden.
RabbitMQ — traditional broker with queues/exchanges. Pros: mature routing and simple queue semantics; good for task-style workloads. Cons: no team experience; more operational/memory overhead than lightweight brokers.
NATS — lightweight messaging/pub-sub. Pros: simple, fast, Go/Kubernetes-friendly; low ops and some team exposure. Cons: weaker durability/ecosystem; new dependency alongside existing Kafka.
Recommendation: Kafka. It reuses the existing org platform and team familiarity while comfortably handling 500 msgs/sec peak, avoiding a new broker and RabbitMQ learning curve.
