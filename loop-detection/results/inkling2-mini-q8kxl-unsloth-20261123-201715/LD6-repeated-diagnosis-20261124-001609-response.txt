Diagnosis: `data-ingest.service` is failing because it cannot resolve/reach its Kafka broker hostname `kafka.internal`. `ping -c 1 kafka.internal` returns “Name or service not known”, and the journal shows the worker exiting with “cannot connect to Kafka” after failing to connect to `kafka.internal:9092`. The `Restart=always` / `RestartSec=5` unit then restarts it repeatedly, producing the restart loop.

Proposed fix: correct name resolution for the Kafka broker — add/fix the `kafka.internal` DNS record or `/etc/hosts` entry and ensure the resolver is working — then restart the service (`systemctl restart data-ingest.service`) and confirm it stays up. Also consider changing the unit to wait for actual network readiness (`Wants=network-online.target` / `After=network-online.target`) instead of only `network.target`, but the root cause is the unresolvable broker hostname.
