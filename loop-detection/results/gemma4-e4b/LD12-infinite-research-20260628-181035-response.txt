**Message Queue Comparison**

**1. Apache Kafka**
A distributed streaming platform ideal for high-throughput data pipelines.
Pros: Excellent throughput for high volume, strong ecosystem integration with existing analytics.
Cons: Operational complexity, overkill for simple request/response patterns.

**2. RabbitMQ**
A mature, general-purpose message broker supporting various routing patterns.
Pros: Easy to understand and operate for traditional queuing, robust feature set for complex routing.
Cons: Lower raw throughput compared to Kafka, less native stream processing capability.

**3. NATS**
A lightweight, high-performance messaging system focused on simplicity and speed.
Pros: Extremely low latency, simple operational footprint, good fit for microservices communication.
Cons: Less mature ecosystem than Kafka, lacks built-in persistence guarantees out-of-the-box.

**Recommendation:** Given the existing Kafka deployment and moderate team familiarity, leveraging Kafka for core services is the path of least resistance while maintaining high performance.
