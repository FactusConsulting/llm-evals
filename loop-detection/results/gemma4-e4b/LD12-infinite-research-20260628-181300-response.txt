**Message Queue Comparison**

**1. Apache Kafka**
A distributed streaming platform ideal for high-throughput data pipelines.
Pros: Excellent throughput for high volume, integrates well with existing analytics stack.
Cons: Operational complexity, overkill for simple request/response patterns.

**2. RabbitMQ**
A mature, general-purpose message broker supporting various routing patterns.
Pros: Easy to understand and operate for traditional queuing, strong community support.
Cons: Lower raw throughput compared to Kafka, less suited for stream processing.

**3. NATS**
A lightweight, high-performance messaging system focused on simplicity and speed.
Pros: Extremely low latency, simple operational footprint, good fit for microservices.
Cons: Less mature ecosystem than Kafka, limited built-in persistence features.

**Recommendation:** NATS is the best fit because its lightweight nature and low latency align well with the existing microservices architecture without introducing the operational overhead of a full Kafka replacement.
