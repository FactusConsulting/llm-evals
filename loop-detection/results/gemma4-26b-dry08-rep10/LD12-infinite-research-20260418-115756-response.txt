**Apache Kafka**
Distributed event streaming platform for enterprise-grade data pipelines.
Pros: Leverages existing organizational expertise; extreme scalability.
Cons: High operational complexity; heavy resource overhead.

**NATS**
Cloud-native messaging system designed specifically for lightweight microservices.
Pros: Minimal resource footprint; seamless Kubernetes compatibility.
Cons: Less robust data persistence; smaller ecosystem.

**Redis Streams**
Append-only log-based messaging built into the Redis ecosystem.
Pros: Extremely low latency; simple architectural management.
Cons: Memory-bound storage capacity; limited advanced routing.

**Recommendation: NATS**
NATS provides the most efficient, Kubernetes-native, and lightweight solution for your specific Go-based service volume.
