**Apache Kafka**
Distributed event streaming platform designed for high-throughput data pipelines.
Pros: Leverages existing organizational infrastructure; proven massive scalability.
Cons: High operational complexity; heavy resource footprint.

**NATS**
Lightweight cloud-native messaging system optimized for speed and simplicity.
Pros: Seamless Kubernetes and Go compatibility; minimal resource requirements.
Cons: Less robust built-in persistence; smaller ecosystem than Kafka.

**RabbitMQ**
Versatile message broker featuring sophisticated routing via AMQP.
Pros: Highly mature feature set; excellent routing flexibility.
Cons: Significant management overhead in Kubernetes; steep learning curve for new teams.

Recommendation: NATS.
Rationale: NATS provides the best alignment with your Go-based stack and Kubernetes environment while remaining lightweight enough for your specific traffic patterns.
