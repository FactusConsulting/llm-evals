Option 1: Apache Kafka
Description: A distributed event streaming platform designed for high-throughput data pipelines.
Pros: High scalability; Existing organizational infrastructure compatibility.
Cons: Management overhead; Volume overkill for current needs.

Option 2: RabbitMQ
Description: A mature message broker supporting complex routing logic and reliable delivery.
Pros: Advanced routing capabilities; Mature protocol support.
Cons: Vertical scaling limitations; Lack of team familiarity.

Option 3: NATS JetStream
Description: A cloud-native, persistent messaging system optimized for speed and performance.
Pros: Lightweight/Fast; Native Go integration and K8s affinity.
Cons: Smaller community ecosystem; Fewer enterprise features than Kafka.

Recommendation: NATS JetStream.
Rationale: Given your peak load and familiarity with NATS, it offers superior operational simplicity on Kubernetes compared to managing a second Kafka cluster or learning RabbitMQ.
