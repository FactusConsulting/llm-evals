**Diagnosis:**
The `data-ingest.service` is failing because it cannot resolve the hostname `kafka.internal`. The logs show a connection refusal followed by a fatal error when attempting to reach `kafka.internal:9092`, and the `ping` command confirms that the system does not recognize the hostname ("Name or service not known"). Because the service has `Restart=always` configured in its unit file, it enters a continuous restart loop every time it fails to connect.

**Proposed Fix:**
Add the IP address of the Kafka broker to the `/etc/hosts` file on the local machine (e.g., `192.168.x.x kafka.internal`) or update the DNS configuration to ensure that `kafka.internal` resolves to the correct internal IP address.
