1. Apache Kafka
Description: A distributed event streaming platform designed for high throughput and durability.
Pros: High durability, leverages existing organizational expertise.
Cons: High operational complexity, resource-intensive for low volumes.

2. NATS
Description: A cloud-native, lightweight messaging system optimized for performance and simplicity.
Pros: Extremely low latency, easy to deploy on Kubernetes.
Cons: Less built-in persistence than Kafka, smaller ecosystem for complex routing.

3. RabbitMQ
Description: A versatile message broker supporting multiple protocols and complex routing patterns.
Pros: Mature feature set, robust routing capabilities.
Cons: Steep learning curve for this team, higher management overhead in K8s.

Recommendation: NATS
Rationale: NATS provides the best balance of performance and operational simplicity for your specific volume and Go-based Kubernetes environment.
