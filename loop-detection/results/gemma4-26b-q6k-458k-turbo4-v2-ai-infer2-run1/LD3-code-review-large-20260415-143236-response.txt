**Issues:**
- `cache/cache.go:27`: The `evict` goroutine is started in `New` but has no mechanism for termination (e.g., a `context.Context` or a `Close()` method). This causes a goroutine leak every time a `Cache` instance is created and eventually discarded.
- `cache/cache.go:49`: The `evict` method holds the mutex `c.mu` for the entire duration of the map iteration. For large caches, this will cause significant latency spikes for all other `Get`, `Set`, and `Delete` operations.

**Nits:**
- `cache/cache.go:47`: The eviction interval is hardcoded to `time.Minute` and should be configurable.
- `cache/cache.go:40`: The expiration check in `Get` is performed after the mutex is released. While safe due to the struct being copied, it is more idiomatic to perform the check while holding the lock.
