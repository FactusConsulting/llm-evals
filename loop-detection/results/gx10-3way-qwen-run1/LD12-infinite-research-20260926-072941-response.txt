Options:

1. Kafka  
Description: Distributed streaming platform with durable logs.  
Pros: Existing org deployment and analytics alignment; high throughput and replayability.  
Cons: Operational complexity for small volume; less suited for simple request/reply or low-latency queues.

2. RabbitMQ  
Description: Mature broker for flexible routing and queues.  
Pros: Strong message routing and delivery guarantees; easier for traditional queue workloads.  
Cons: No team experience; scaling and HA require careful design.

3. NATS JetStream  
Description: Lightweight cloud-native messaging with optional persistence.  
Pros: Kubernetes-friendly and low operational overhead; good fit for Go services and moderate volume.  
Cons: Less mature than Kafka/RabbitMQ; team has only some exposure.

Recommendation: Use NATS JetStream for new microservices messaging unless requirements demand Kafka-style streaming analytics.  
Rationale: It meets 50k/day and 500/sec with simpler Kubernetes operations while preserving Go-friendly APIs and avoiding RabbitMQ learning curve.
