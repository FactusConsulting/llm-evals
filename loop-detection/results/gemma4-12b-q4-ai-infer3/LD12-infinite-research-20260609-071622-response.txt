**Apache Kafka**
Description: A distributed streaming platform designed for high throughput and fault tolerance.
Pros: High scalability; native integration with existing analytics stack.
Cons: Complex operational overhead; higher latency at low volumes.

**RabbitMQ**
Description: A traditional message broker supporting multiple protocols like AMQP and MQTT.
Pros: Advanced routing capabilities; mature ecosystem.
Cons: Vertical scaling limits; complex management of large queues.

**NATS**
Description: A lightweight, cloud-native messaging system optimized for speed and simplicity.
Pros: Native Kubernetes compatibility; extremely low latency.
Cons: Limited persistence features; smaller community support.

Recommendation: Apache Kafka.
Rationale: Leveraging your organization's current Kafka investment minimizes migration costs while meeting all volume requirements.
