1. Apache Kafka
Description: A distributed event streaming platform for high-throughput data pipelines.
Pros: High durability, leverages existing organizational expertise.
Cons: High operational complexity, resource-intensive for low volumes.

2. NATS
Description: A cloud-native, high-performance messaging system for lightweight microservices.
Pros: Extremely low latency, easy to deploy on Kubernetes.
Cons: Limited built-in persistence, smaller ecosystem for complex routing.

3. RabbitMQ
Description: A versatile message broker supporting complex routing patterns.
Pros: Mature feature set, excellent routing capabilities.
Cons: New learning curve for the team, higher management overhead.

Recommendation: NATS
Rationale: NATS provides the most efficient performance-to-complexity ratio for your specific message volume and Kubernetes-centric environment.
