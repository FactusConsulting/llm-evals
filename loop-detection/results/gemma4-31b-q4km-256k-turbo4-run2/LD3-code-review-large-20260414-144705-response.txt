- `cache/cache.go:23`: Goroutine leak. The `evict` goroutine started in `New` has no termination mechanism (e.g., a stop channel or context), meaning the goroutine will leak every time a `Cache` instance is garbage collected.
- `cache/cache.go:51-58`: Performance/Blocking. The `evict` method holds the mutex lock while iterating over the entire map. For large caches, this will block all `Get`, `Set`, and `Delete` operations for the duration of the cleanup.
