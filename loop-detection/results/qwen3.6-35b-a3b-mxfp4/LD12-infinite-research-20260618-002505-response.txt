**Apache Kafka**
Description: Distributed event streaming platform optimized for high-throughput log aggregation.
Pros: Proven scalability; existing org infrastructure reduces setup overhead.
Cons: Heavy resource footprint; complex operational maintenance.

**RabbitMQ**
Description: Feature-rich message broker supporting advanced routing and reliable delivery.
Pros: Excellent reliability guarantees; mature ecosystem with plugins.
Cons: Steep learning curve for the team; higher latency under burst loads.

**NATS**
Description: Lightweight, high-performance messaging system designed for cloud-native environments.
Pros: Minimal resource usage; native Go client with excellent K8s integration.
Cons: Limited advanced routing features; smaller community than Kafka/RabbitMQ.

Recommendation: NATS JetStream.
Rationale: It aligns best with your Go/Kubernetes stack, matches the moderate message volume, and leverages your team’s existing exposure while avoiding Kafka’s operational overhead.
