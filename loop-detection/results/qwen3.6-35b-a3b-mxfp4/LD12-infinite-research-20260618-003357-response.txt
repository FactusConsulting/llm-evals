**Apache Kafka**
Description: Distributed event streaming platform optimized for high-throughput data pipelines.
Pros: Leverages existing org infrastructure and team familiarity; battle-tested reliability.
Cons: Heavy resource footprint; complex operational overhead for low-volume workloads.

**NATS JetStream**
Description: Lightweight, cloud-native messaging system with built-in persistence and streaming.
Pros: Minimal K8s resource usage; simple Go client integration and low latency.
Cons: Smaller ecosystem than Kafka; limited advanced stream processing features.

**RabbitMQ**
Description: Mature broker implementing AMQP with flexible routing and reliable delivery.
Pros: Excellent message routing flexibility; strong community support and plugins.
Cons: Requires new team training; higher memory consumption under sustained load.

Recommendation: NATS JetStream.
Rationale: It aligns best with your Go stack, K8s environment, and moderate traffic while minimizing operational overhead and leveraging your existing exposure.
