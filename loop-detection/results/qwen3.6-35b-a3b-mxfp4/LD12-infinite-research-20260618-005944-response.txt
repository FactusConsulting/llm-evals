**Kafka**
Description: Distributed event streaming platform optimized for high-throughput log aggregation.
Pros: Leverages existing org infrastructure and team familiarity; proven durability.
Cons: Heavy operational overhead; architecturally overkill for low-volume workloads.

**NATS JetStream**
Description: Lightweight, cloud-native messaging system with built-in persistence and pub/sub capabilities.
Pros: Minimal resource footprint; excellent Kubernetes integration and native Go client support.
Cons: Smaller ecosystem; lacks advanced stream processing tooling.

**RabbitMQ**
Description: Mature broker implementing AMQP with flexible routing and reliable delivery guarantees.
Pros: Rich routing capabilities; extensive plugin ecosystem and community support.
Cons: Requires new team training; higher memory consumption under sustained load.

Recommendation: NATS JetStream.
Rationale: Its lightweight architecture, native Go support, and low operational overhead align perfectly with your moderate message volume and Kubernetes environment, avoiding Kafka’s bloat while sidestepping RabbitMQ’s learning curve.
