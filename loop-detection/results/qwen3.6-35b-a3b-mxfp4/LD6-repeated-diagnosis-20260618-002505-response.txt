**Diagnosis:** The service is trapped in a restart loop because it cannot resolve the hostname `kafka.internal` at startup. The unit file only specifies `After=network.target`, which is a weak ordering dependency that does not guarantee DNS resolution or full network connectivity is available. The application fails immediately, systemd restarts it, and the cycle repeats.

**Proposed Fix:** Update the `[Unit]` section of `/etc/systemd/system/data-ingest.service` to depend on `network-online.target` instead, ensuring DNS and network interfaces are fully ready before the service launches:
```ini
[Unit]
Description=Data Ingest Worker
After=network-online.target
Wants=network-online.target
```
Apply the change with `systemctl daemon-reload` and `systemctl restart data-ingest.service`.
