**Kafka**
Description: Distributed event streaming platform optimized for high-throughput log aggregation.
Pros: Leverages existing org infrastructure; strong durability and exactly-once semantics.
Cons: High operational overhead in K8s; overkill for 500 msgs/sec peak.

**NATS JetStream**
Description: Lightweight, cloud-native messaging system with built-in persistence and pub/sub capabilities.
Pros: Minimal resource footprint; excellent Go client support and K8s deployment simplicity.
Cons: Smaller ecosystem than Kafka; limited advanced stream processing features.

**RabbitMQ**
Description: Mature, feature-rich message broker supporting complex routing and reliable queuing.
Pros: Flexible routing and exchange patterns; battle-tested reliability for microservices.
Cons: Requires Erlang runtime overhead; team lacks prior experience, increasing onboarding time.

Recommendation: NATS JetStream
Rationale: It aligns best with your Go stack, low message volume, and Kubernetes environment while minimizing operational complexity and leveraging your team’s existing exposure.
