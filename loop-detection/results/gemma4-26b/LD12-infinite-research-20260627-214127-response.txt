**1. Apache Kafka**
A distributed event streaming platform designed for high-throughput and fault tolerance.
*   **Pros:** Leverages existing organizational expertise; excellent for high-volume data retention.
*   **Cons:** High operational complexity; significant resource overhead for moderate volumes.

**2. RabbitMQ**
A versatile, traditional message broker supporting complex routing patterns.
*   **Pros:** Mature feature set with robust routing capabilities; widely supported client libraries.
*   **Cons:** Steep learning curve for teams without prior experience; harder to scale horizontally compared to cloud-native alternatives.

**3. NATS**
A lightweight, cloud-native messaging system optimized for performance and simplicity.
*   **Pros:** Extremely low latency and minimal resource footprint; native fit for Kubernetes environments.
*   **Cons:** Less robust built-in persistence compared to Kafka; smaller ecosystem of advanced enterprise features.

**RECOMMENDATION**
NATS

**RATIONALE**
Given your moderate message volume and Kubernetes environment, NATS provides the best balance of performance and operational simplicity without the heavy overhead of Kafka.
