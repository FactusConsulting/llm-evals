**1. Apache Kafka**
A distributed event streaming platform designed for high-throughput and durability.
*   **Pros:** Leverages existing organizational expertise; excellent for high-volume data retention.
*   **Cons:** High operational complexity; significant resource overhead for moderate volumes.

**2. RabbitMQ**
A traditional message broker supporting complex routing patterns via AMQP.
*   **Pros:** Mature feature set for complex routing; lightweight compared to Kafka.
*   **Cons:** Steep learning curve for a team with no prior experience; harder to scale horizontally in K8s.

**3. NATS JetStream**
A cloud-native, lightweight messaging system built for high-performance microservices.
*   **Pros:** Extremely low operational overhead in Kubernetes; native Go compatibility.
*   **Cons:** Smaller ecosystem compared to Kafka; less robust tooling for long-term data analytics.

**RECOMMENDATION**
NATS JetStream.

**RATIONALE**
Given your moderate message volume and existing NATS exposure, NATS JetStream provides the best balance of low operational complexity and high performance without the heavy overhead of your existing Kafka deployment.
