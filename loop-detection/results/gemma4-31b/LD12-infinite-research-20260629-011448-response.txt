**1. Apache Kafka**
Distributed streaming platform designed for high-throughput and persistence.
- Pros: Already deployed in org; extreme scalability.
- Cons: High operational complexity; overkill for 50k msgs/day.

**2. RabbitMQ**
Versatile traditional message broker supporting multiple messaging patterns.
- Pros: Mature ecosystem; flexible routing options.
- Cons: No team experience; higher resource overhead than NATS.

**3. NATS JetStream**
Cloud-native messaging system built for performance and simplicity.
- Pros: Lightweight K8s deployment; team has existing exposure.
- Cons: Smaller community than Kafka; fewer built-in enterprise plugins.

**Recommendation: NATS JetStream**
Rationale: It provides the best balance of performance and operational simplicity for your specific volume while leveraging existing team familiarity.
