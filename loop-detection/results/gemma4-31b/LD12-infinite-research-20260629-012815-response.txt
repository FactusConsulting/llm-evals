**1. Apache Kafka**
Distributed streaming platform designed for high-throughput and persistence.
- Pros: Already deployed in org; extreme scalability.
- Cons: High operational overhead; overkill for current volume.

**2. RabbitMQ**
Versatile traditional message broker supporting multiple protocols.
- Pros: Mature routing capabilities; strong delivery guarantees.
- Cons: No team experience; complex clustering on Kubernetes.

**3. NATS JetStream**
Cloud-native messaging system built for performance and simplicity.
- Pros: Lightweight K8s deployment; team has existing exposure.
- Cons: Smaller ecosystem than Kafka; fewer advanced routing patterns.

**Recommendation: NATS JetStream**
Given the low message volume and existing team exposure, NATS provides the best balance of performance and operational simplicity without the overhead of Kafka.
