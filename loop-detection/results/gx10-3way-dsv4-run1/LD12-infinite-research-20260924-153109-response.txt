**Option 1: Kafka**  
Distributed log-based streaming platform already deployed for analytics.  
Pros: Existing operational expertise; strong durability and replay.  
Cons: Heavyweight for low volume; adds operational overhead.

**Option 2: RabbitMQ**  
Mature AMQP broker with flexible routing and queueing.  
Pros: Rich routing features; well-known patterns.  
Cons: No in-house experience; requires new ops learning.

**Option 3: NATS**  
Lightweight, high-performance messaging with simple pub/sub and request-reply.  
Pros: Low latency, small footprint; Go-native client and team exposure.  
Cons: Limited persistence/streaming; weaker ordering guarantees.

**Recommendation:** NATS, because it matches the low volume, Go-centric stack, and team's existing exposure while avoiding Kafka's operational weight.
