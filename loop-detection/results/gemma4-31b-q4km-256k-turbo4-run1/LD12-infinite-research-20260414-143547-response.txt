**Apache Kafka**
Description: Distributed event streaming platform.
Pros: High throughput, existing organizational infrastructure.
Cons: High operational overhead, complex configuration.

**NATS**
Description: Lightweight, cloud-native messaging system.
Pros: Extremely fast, simple Kubernetes deployment.
Cons: Limited persistence options, smaller ecosystem.

**RabbitMQ**
Description: Traditional enterprise message broker.
Pros: Flexible routing, mature feature set.
Cons: Higher resource usage, team learning curve.

Recommendation: Apache Kafka.
Rationale: Leveraging existing organizational infrastructure reduces operational overhead and deployment time.
