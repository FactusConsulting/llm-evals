**Concrete Issues**
- `cache/cache.go:23`: Goroutine leak. The `evict` goroutine started in `New` has no termination mechanism (e.g., a stop channel or context), meaning the goroutine will leak every time a `Cache` instance is garbage collected.
- `cache/cache.go:48-57`: Performance/Blocking. The `evict` function holds the mutex lock for the entire duration of the map iteration. In a large cache, this will block all `Get`, `Set`, and `Delete` operations until the entire map has been scanned.

**Nits**
- `cache/cache.go:10, 18, 27, 33`: Use `any` instead of `interface{}` for Go 1.18+ compatibility.
- `cache/cache.go:49`: The 1-minute sleep interval is hardcoded; consider making the eviction frequency configurable or tying it to the `ttl`.
