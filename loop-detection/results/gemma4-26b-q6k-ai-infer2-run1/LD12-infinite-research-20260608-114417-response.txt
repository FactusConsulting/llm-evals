**Apache Kafka**
Description: A distributed event streaming platform designed for high-throughput data pipelines.
Pros: Leverages existing organizational expertise, massive scalability.
Cons: High operational complexity, significant resource consumption.

**NATS**
Description: A lightweight, cloud-native messaging system optimized for performance and simplicity.
Pros: Extremely low latency, seamless Kubernetes integration.
Cons: Limited advanced persistence features, smaller ecosystem.

**RabbitMQ**
Description: A mature message broker supporting complex routing patterns via AMQP.
Pros: Highly flexible routing logic, extensive feature set.
Cons: Higher memory footprint, steep learning curve for new teams.

Recommendation: NATS.
Rationale: Its minimal operational overhead and excellent Kubernetes compatibility align perfectly with your current scale and Go-centric environment.
