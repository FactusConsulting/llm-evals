**Diagnosis:**
The `data-ingest` service is repeatedly crashing because it fails to establish a connection with its dependency, Kafka, at `kafka.internal:9092`. Analysis of the logs shows "connection refused," but the subsequent `ping` command reveals that the hostname `kafka.internal: Name or service not known`, indicating a fundamental DNS resolution failure preventing the service from even attempting a successful TCP connection.

**Proposed Fix:**
Correct the DNS configuration on the host machine so that the hostname `kafka.internal.9092` resolves to the correct IP address of the Kafka broker.
