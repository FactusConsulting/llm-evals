Diagnosis: The `data-ingest.service` is repeatedly failing because the application cannot connect to the Kafka broker at `kafka.internal:9092`. Further investigation shows that the system cannot resolve the hostname `kafka.internal` (DNS resolution failure).

Proposed Fix: Update the system's DNS configuration or add an entry for `kafka.internal` to `/etc/hosts` so that the hostname can be correctly resolved by the system before the application attempts to connect.
