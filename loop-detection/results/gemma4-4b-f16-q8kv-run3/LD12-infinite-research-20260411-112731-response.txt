**Option 1: Apache Kafka**
Description: A distributed streaming platform ideal for high-throughput data pipelines.
Pros: Excellent scalability for high volume; Integrates well with existing organizational deployment.
Cons: Operational complexity is high; Overkill for simple request/response patterns.

**Option 2: RabbitMQ**
Description: A mature, general-purpose message broker supporting various routing patterns.
Pros: Easy to understand for developers new to queuing; Robust feature set for complex routing.
Cons: Can struggle with extreme throughput compared to Kafka; Requires careful cluster management in K8s.

**Option 3: NATS**
Description: A lightweight, high-performance messaging system focused on simplicity and speed.
Pros: Extremely low latency and operational simplicity; Excellent fit for Go services.
Cons: Less mature ecosystem than Kafka/RabbitMQ; Lacks built-in persistence guarantees by default.

Recommendation: NATS is the best fit because its lightweight nature and simplicity align well with the existing Go stack and moderate operational overhead tolerance.
