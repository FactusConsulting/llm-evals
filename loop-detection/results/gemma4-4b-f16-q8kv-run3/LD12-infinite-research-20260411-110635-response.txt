**Option 1: Apache Kafka**
Description: A distributed streaming platform designed for high-throughput data pipelines.
Pros: Excellent scalability for high volume; integrates well with existing organizational Kafka deployment.
Cons: Operational complexity is high; overkill for simple request/response patterns.

**Option 2: RabbitMQ**
Description: A mature, general-purpose message broker supporting various routing patterns.
Pros: Easy to understand and moderate learning curve; robust feature set for complex routing.
Cons: Can struggle with extremely high sustained throughput compared to Kafka; requires dedicated cluster management.

**Option 3: NATS**
Description: A lightweight, high-performance messaging system focused on simplicity and speed.
Pros: Extremely low latency and simple deployment on Kubernetes; excellent for Go services.
Cons: Less mature ecosystem for complex persistence compared to Kafka; limited built-in queue semantics.

Recommendation: NATS is the best fit because its lightweight nature and simplicity align well with the team's moderate familiarity and the moderate message volume requirements.
