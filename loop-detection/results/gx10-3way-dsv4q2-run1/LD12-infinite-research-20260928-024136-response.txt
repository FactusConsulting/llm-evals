1. **Kafka** — Distributed streaming platform for high-throughput pub/sub and event sourcing.  
   Pros: Existing org expertise and infrastructure; strong durability and replayability.  
   Cons: Overkill for current volume; operational complexity on Kubernetes.

2. **NATS** — Lightweight, high-performance messaging system with simple pub/sub and request-reply.  
   Pros: Simple Go-native client; low latency and low resource footprint.  
   Cons: Limited persistence (JetStream still maturing); team has only some exposure.

3. **RabbitMQ** — Mature message broker with flexible routing and plugin ecosystem.  
   Pros: Rich routing features; good Kubernetes operator support.  
   Cons: No existing team experience; adds operational overhead.

**Recommendation:** NATS, because its lightweight footprint and Go-native client fit your Kubernetes platform and low message volume, while leveraging your team's existing NATS exposure.
