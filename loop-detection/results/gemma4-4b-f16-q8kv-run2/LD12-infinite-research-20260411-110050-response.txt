**Option 1: Apache Kafka**
Description: A distributed streaming platform designed for high-throughput data pipelines.
Pros: Excellent scalability for high volume; integrates well with existing organizational Kafka deployment.
Cons: Operational complexity is high; overkill for simple request/response patterns.

**Option 2: RabbitMQ**
Description: A mature, general-purpose message broker supporting various queuing models.
Pros: Excellent developer tooling and ease of use for traditional queuing; strong community support.
Cons: Scaling horizontally can be more complex than Kafka; less suited for stream processing.

**Option 3: NATS**
Description: A lightweight, high-performance messaging system focused on simplicity and speed.
Pros: Extremely low latency and simple deployment on Kubernetes; good fit for Go services.
Cons: Lacks built-in persistence features of Kafka; less mature ecosystem for complex routing.

Recommendation: NATS is the best fit because its lightweight nature and simplicity align well with the existing Go services and moderate operational overhead tolerance.
