**Option 1: Apache Kafka**
A distributed streaming platform ideal for high-throughput data pipelines.
Pros: Excellent scalability for high volume; Integrates well with existing organizational deployment.
Cons: Operational complexity is high; Can be overkill for simple request/response patterns.

**Option 2: RabbitMQ**
A mature, general-purpose message broker supporting various queuing models.
Pros: Excellent developer tooling and ease of use for traditional queuing; Supports complex routing patterns easily.
Cons: Scaling horizontally can be more complex than Kafka; Lower raw throughput ceiling than Kafka.

**Option 3: NATS**
A lightweight, high-performance messaging system focused on simplicity and speed.
Pros: Extremely low latency and simple operational footprint; Excellent fit for Go services due to lightweight nature.
Cons: Less mature ecosystem for complex stream processing compared to Kafka; Queue semantics are less rigid than RabbitMQ.

**Recommendation:** NATS is recommended because its lightweight nature and simplicity align best with the existing Go service stack and moderate operational familiarity, offering high performance without the overhead of Kafka or RabbitMQ.
