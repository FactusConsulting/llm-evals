**Option 1: Apache Kafka**
A distributed streaming platform designed for high-throughput data pipelines.
Pros: Excellent scalability for high volume; Integrates well with existing organizational Kafka deployment.
Cons: Operational complexity is high; Overkill for low-volume, simple task queuing.

**Option 2: RabbitMQ**
A mature, general-purpose message broker supporting various queuing patterns.
Pros: Simple to understand for teams new to advanced brokers; Robust routing capabilities.
Cons: Can struggle with extreme throughput compared to Kafka; Requires careful cluster management.

**Option 3: NATS**
A lightweight, high-performance messaging system focused on simplicity and speed.
Pros: Extremely low latency and simple deployment on Kubernetes; Excellent for service discovery patterns.
Cons: Less feature-rich for complex persistence needs; Ecosystem maturity is smaller than Kafka.

**Recommendation:** NATS is recommended because its lightweight nature and simplicity align best with the team's moderate familiarity while providing superior performance for the specified message volume.
