**Option 1: Apache Kafka**
Description: A distributed streaming platform designed for high-throughput data pipelines.
Pros: Excellent scalability; Integrates well with existing organizational Kafka deployment.
Cons: Operational complexity; Overkill for low-volume task queuing.

**Option 2: RabbitMQ**
Description: A mature, general-purpose message broker supporting complex routing patterns.
Pros: Mature ecosystem; Excellent support for traditional queuing patterns (AMQP).
Cons: Can struggle with extreme throughput compared to Kafka/NATS; Requires learning curve for the team.

**Option 3: NATS**
Description: A lightweight, high-performance messaging system focused on simplicity and speed.
Pros: Extremely low latency; Simple operational footprint, aligning with existing team exposure.
Cons: Less feature-rich for complex persistence/retries out-of-the-box; Less established enterprise tooling than Kafka.

Recommendation: NATS is the best fit because its lightweight nature and simplicity minimize operational overhead while meeting the current moderate volume requirements.
