**Option 1: Apache Kafka**
A distributed streaming platform for high-throughput data pipelines.
Pros: Excellent integration with existing organizational Kafka deployment; handles high volume reliably.
Cons: Operational complexity is high; overkill for simple request/response patterns.

**Option 2: RabbitMQ**
A mature, general-purpose message broker supporting various protocols.
Pros: Excellent developer tooling and ease of use for traditional queuing; strong community support.
Cons: Can struggle with extreme throughput compared to Kafka/NATS; requires learning a new paradigm.

**Option 3: NATS**
A lightweight, high-performance messaging system focused on simplicity and speed.
Pros: Extremely low latency and simple deployment on Kubernetes; good fit for Go services.
Cons: Less feature-rich for complex stream processing than Kafka; persistence requires external configuration.

Recommendation: NATS is the best fit because it offers high performance and simplicity, aligning well with the team's existing Go familiarity while avoiding the operational overhead of a second Kafka cluster.
