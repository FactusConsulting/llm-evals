

1. Apache Kafka
Distributed event streaming platform.
Pros: Leverages existing org deployment; Handles massive scale.
Cons: High operational overhead; Overkill for current volume.

2. NATS JetStream
Cloud-native messaging system with persistence.
Pros: Go-native client support; Lightweight K8s footprint.
Cons: Smaller community than Kafka; Less mature tooling.

3. RabbitMQ
Robust open-source message broker.
Pros: Flexible routing patterns; Mature stability.
Cons: No team experience; Heavier resource usage than NATS.

Recommendation: NATS JetStream.
Rationale: It aligns with the Go stack and K8s environment while avoiding the operational bloat of Kafka for low-volume traffic.
