

**1. Apache Kafka**
Distributed event streaming platform.
Pros: Leverages existing org deployment; Handles massive scale.
Cons: High operational overhead; Overkill for current volume.

**2. NATS JetStream**
Cloud-native messaging with persistence.
Pros: Go-native client support; Lightweight K8s footprint.
Cons: Smaller ecosystem than Kafka; Less mature tooling.

**3. RabbitMQ**
Advanced message broker with flexible routing.
Pros: Mature protocol support; Easy management UI.
Cons: No team experience; Higher latency than NATS.

**Recommendation:** NATS JetStream.
**Rationale:** Aligns with Go stack and team exposure while avoiding Kafka's operational bloat for low-volume needs.
