**Option 1: Apache Kafka**
- Distributed, high-throughput log-based messaging system.
- Pros: Strong durability & ordering guarantees; already deployed in the org for analytics.
- Cons: Higher operational complexity; overkill for modest 50k/day volume.

**Option 2: RabbitMQ**
- Feature-rich, AMQP-based message broker with flexible routing.
- Pros: Mature ecosystem & management UI; supports complex routing patterns.
- Cons: Steeper learning curve for the team; requires additional resources for HA setup.

**Option 3: NATS**
- Lightweight, high‑performance pub/sub system focused on simplicity.
- Pros: Low latency & minimal resource usage; easy to deploy in Kubernetes.
- Cons: Lacks built‑in persistence (unless using NATS Streaming); fewer advanced features.

**Recommendation:** Choose NATS for its simplicity, low overhead, and good fit with existing Go services, while leveraging Kafka only for analytics‑specific streams.
