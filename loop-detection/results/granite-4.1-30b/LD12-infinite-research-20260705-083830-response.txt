**Option 1: Apache Kafka**
- Distributed, high-throughput log-based messaging system.
- Pros: Scalable to high volumes, strong durability guarantees, mature ecosystem.
- Cons: Complex operational overhead, steep learning curve for non‑Kafka teams.

**Option 2: RabbitMQ**
- Feature‑rich message broker supporting multiple protocols (AMQP, MQTT, etc.).
- Pros: Flexible routing patterns, extensive management UI, good for request‑reply workflows.
- Cons: Higher latency than log‑based systems, requires additional operational expertise.

**Option 3: NATS**
- Lightweight, high‑performance pub/sub system focused on simplicity.
- Pros: Low latency, minimal resource footprint, easy to deploy in Kubernetes.
- Cons: Limited built‑in persistence (unless using NATS Streaming/JetStream), fewer advanced features.

**Recommendation:** Use **NATS** (with JetStream for persistence) because it aligns with the team's moderate familiarity, offers low latency suitable for 500 msgs/sec peaks, and integrates cleanly with existing Kubernetes deployments while avoiding Kafka's operational complexity.
