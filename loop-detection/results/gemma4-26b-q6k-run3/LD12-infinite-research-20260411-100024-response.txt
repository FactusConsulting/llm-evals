1. Apache Kafka
Description: A distributed event streaming platform for high-throughput data pipelines.
Pros: High durability, leverages existing organizational expertise.
Cons: High operational complexity, resource-intensive for low volumes.

2. NATS
Description: A cloud-native, lightweight messaging system optimized for performance and simplicity.
Pros: Extremely low latency, native Go support.
Cons: Limited built-in persistence, smaller ecosystem for complex routing.

3. RabbitMQ
Description: A versatile message broker implementing AMQP for complex routing logic.
Pros: Mature feature set, excellent protocol support.
Cons: Steep learning curve, higher management overhead in K8s.

Recommendation: NATS.
Rationale: NATS provides the best balance of performance and operational simplicity for your Go-based Kubernetes environment given your current message volume.
