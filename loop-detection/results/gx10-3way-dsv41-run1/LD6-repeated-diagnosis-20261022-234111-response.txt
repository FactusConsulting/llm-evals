**Diagnosis:** `data-ingest.service` is failing because the hostname `kafka.internal` cannot be resolved. The application exits with status 1 when it cannot connect to Kafka, and systemd’s `Restart=always` causes the repeated restart loop. The `ping` failure (`Name or service not known`) confirms a DNS/name-resolution problem rather than a simple Kafka outage.

**Proposed fix:** Correct name resolution for `kafka.internal` — either by fixing the DNS configuration or by adding the correct broker IP mapping in `/etc/hosts` — then restart `data-ingest.service`. Optionally, adjust the service’s restart policy if immediate endless restarts are undesirable, but the primary fix is restoring resolution/reachability for `kafka.internal:9092`.
