**Kafka**
- Distributed, partitioned log for high-throughput event streaming.
- Pros: Already deployed in the org; mature Go clients (franz-go) and ecosystem.
- Cons: Operationally heavy for 50k msgs/day; consumer-group/offset complexity is overkill for microservice request/reply.

**NATS with JetStream**
- Lightweight cloud-native messaging with optional persistence via JetStream.
- Pros: Simple Go client and tiny Kubernetes footprint; supports both pub/sub and request/reply patterns.
- Cons: Team has only partial exposure; smaller ecosystem and fewer managed offerings than Kafka.

**RabbitMQ**
- Mature AMQP broker with flexible exchange/queue routing.
- Pros: Rich routing and per-message acknowledgements; straightforward model for task queues.
- Cons: No team experience; Erlang-based clustering adds learning/ops cost; less natural for Go/Kubernetes streaming patterns.

**Recommendation:** NATS with JetStream — at this modest volume and with microservice request/reply needs, it offers the lightest Go-friendly operational footprint, while the existing Kafka deployment can continue serving analytics.
