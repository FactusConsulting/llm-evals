### Message Queue Comparison

**1. Apache Kafka**
A distributed event streaming platform designed for high-throughput and data persistence.
*   **Pros:** High durability; leverages existing organizational expertise.
*   **Cons:** High operational complexity; resource-intensive for low volumes.

**2. NATS JetStream**
A cloud-native, lightweight messaging system built for high-performance microservices.
*   **Pros:** Extremely low latency; easy to deploy on Kubernetes.
*   **Cons:** Smaller ecosystem than Kafka; less mature tooling for complex persistence.

**3. RabbitMQ**
A traditional message broker focused on complex routing and reliable delivery.
*   **Pros:** Highly flexible routing patterns; mature feature set.
*   **Cons:** Steep learning curve for the team; higher management overhead in K8s.

**Recommendation:** NATS JetStream
**Rationale:** Given your moderate NATS exposure and relatively low message volume, NATS JetStream provides the best balance of performance and operational simplicity without the heavy overhead of Kafka.
