**Key Risks & Prerequisites**
- *Risks*: Brief service interruption during failover; configuration drift between PG 14/16 (deprecated parameters, `pg_hba.conf` changes); extension compatibility gaps; connection pool exhaustion during switchover; rollback complexity if data corruption occurs.
- *Prerequisites*: Full automated backup with verified point-in-time recovery; staging environment mirroring production topology; updated connection strings and health check endpoints; rollback runbook; approved maintenance window; monitoring/alerting configured for replication lag and connection errors.

**Migration Plan**
1. Provision a staging Kubernetes namespace and deploy the payments service pointing to a cloned PG 14 instance.
2. Execute the full test suite against staging, validating transaction isolation, extension behavior, and psycopg2 2.9.3 compatibility.
3. Audit all custom `postgresql.conf` settings, extensions, and roles; map deprecated parameters to PG 16 equivalents.
4. Coordinate with DigitalOcean support to schedule the upgrade and confirm cross-region clone or in-place upgrade paths. [Requires external coordination & maintenance window approval]
5. Trigger a production backup and verify restore integrity in an isolated sandbox. [Requires security/compliance sign-off]
6. Update the CI/CD pipeline to build a canary release with PG 16 connection parameters and exponential backoff retry logic.
7. Deploy the canary to 20% of pods and validate health checks, query latency, and error rates against PG 14.
8. Initiate the DigitalOcean DBaaS upgrade to PG 16 during the approved maintenance window. [Requires on-call engineer approval]
9. Monitor replication sync, cluster health, and query performance until the primary node reports healthy status.
10. Update the Kubernetes Service DNS and load balancer rules to route traffic to the upgraded PG 16 instance. [Requires platform team approval]
11. Run automated integration tests against the live database to confirm transactional integrity and payment processing.
12. Scale down canary pods and promote the full payments service deployment to PG 16.
13. Verify monitoring dashboards, alert thresholds, and automated backup schedules are aligned with PG 16 metrics.
14. Archive the PG 14 instance after 72 hours of stable operation and zero critical incidents.

**Approval & Coordination Requirements**
- Step 4: DigitalOcean support coordination and formal maintenance window approval.
- Step 5: Security/compliance sign-off for backup retention and restore validation.
- Step 8: On-call engineer approval to proceed with live upgrade.
- Step 10: Platform team approval for DNS/load balancer routing changes.
