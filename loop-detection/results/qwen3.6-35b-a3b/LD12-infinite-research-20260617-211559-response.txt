NATS JetStream
Description: Lightweight, cloud-native streaming built on the high-performance NATS protocol.
Pros: Extremely low latency; native Kubernetes operator support and excellent Go client library.
Cons: Smaller ecosystem than Kafka; limited advanced stream processing features.

RabbitMQ
Description: Mature, feature-rich broker supporting multiple messaging patterns and protocols.
Pros: Rich routing capabilities; extensive plugin ecosystem and strong community support.
Cons: Higher resource overhead; complex clustering management in Kubernetes.

Apache Kafka
Description: Distributed event streaming platform optimized for high-throughput, durable logs.
Pros: Battle-tested durability; leverages existing org infrastructure and powerful stream tools.
Cons: Heavy operational footprint; overkill for current volume and steep learning curve.

Recommendation: NATS JetStream. Rationale: It aligns perfectly with your Go stack and Kubernetes environment while avoiding the operational overhead of Kafka and the complexity of RabbitMQ for your current message volume.
