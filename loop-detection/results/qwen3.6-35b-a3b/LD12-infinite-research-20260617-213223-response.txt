**NATS JetStream**
Lightweight, cloud-native streaming built on the high-performance NATS core.
Top 2 Pros: Extremely low latency; native Kubernetes operator support and excellent Go client library.
Top 2 Cons: Smaller ecosystem than Kafka; limited advanced stream processing features.

**RabbitMQ**
Mature, feature-rich broker specializing in flexible routing and reliable delivery.
Top 2 Pros: Rich exchange routing capabilities; extensive plugin ecosystem and strong community support.
Top 2 Cons: Higher resource overhead; lacks built-in persistent log semantics and idiomatic Go client.

**Kafka**
Distributed event streaming platform optimized for high-throughput, durable logs.
Top 2 Pros: Battle-tested durability; leverages existing org infrastructure and powerful stream processing tools.
Top 2 Cons: Heavy operational footprint; overkill for current volume and steep learning curve.

Recommendation: NATS JetStream.
Rationale: It aligns perfectly with your Go stack and Kubernetes environment while avoiding the operational overhead of Kafka for your current message volume.
