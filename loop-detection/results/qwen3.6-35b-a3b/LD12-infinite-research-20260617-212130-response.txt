**NATS JetStream**
Description: Lightweight, cloud-native streaming built on the high-performance NATS protocol.
Pros: Extremely low latency and resource footprint; excellent Go client support and K8s operator maturity.
Cons: Smaller ecosystem than Kafka; limited built-in stream processing tools.

**RabbitMQ**
Description: Mature, feature-rich broker specializing in flexible routing and reliable message delivery.
Pros: Highly configurable routing via exchanges; extensive plugin ecosystem and community support.
Cons: Higher memory/CPU overhead; lacks native distributed streaming capabilities.

**Apache Kafka**
Description: Distributed event streaming platform optimized for high-throughput, fault-tolerant log storage.
Pros: Battle-tested durability and horizontal scalability; rich ecosystem of connectors and stream processors.
Cons: Complex operational overhead; overkill for moderate message volumes.

Recommendation: NATS JetStream.
Rationale: It aligns perfectly with your Go/Kubernetes stack, handles your volume efficiently with minimal operational overhead, and leverages your team’s existing NATS familiarity while avoiding Kafka’s complexity for this use case.
