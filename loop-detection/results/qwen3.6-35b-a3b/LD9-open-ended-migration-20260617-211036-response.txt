**Key Risks & Prerequisites**
- **Risks:** psycopg2 2.9.3 lacks native PG 16 protocol support, risking connection drops under load; DigitalOcean DBaaS upgrades may enforce a brief maintenance window or require manual failover; replica lag during promotion can cause read failures; K8s connection pools may exhaust during traffic redirection; undocumented syntax changes or deprecated extensions in PG 16 may break queries.
- **Prerequisites:** Verified full snapshot backup of primary and replica; application containerized with psycopg2 2.9.9+; DigitalOcean maintenance window confirmed; K8s deployment configured with connection retry logic, pool limits, and graceful shutdown hooks; schema and extension audit completed against PG 16 release notes; runbook reviewed by platform engineering; load balancer health checks temporarily disabled to prevent premature traffic routing.

**Migration Plan**
1. [External Coordination] Schedule a maintenance window with DigitalOcean support and align with internal on-call teams.
2. [Human Approval] Submit the runbook to the platform engineering lead and security team for formal sign-off.
3. Drain active connections by scaling the K8s deployment to zero replicas and waiting for all pods to terminate.
4. Trigger a manual snapshot backup of the DigitalOcean PG 14 primary and replica instances.
5. Update the application Dockerfile to install psycopg2 2.9.9+ and rebuild the container image.
6. Push the updated image to the registry and update the K8s deployment manifest with the new image tag.
7. Initiate the PG 14 to 16 upgrade through the DigitalOcean control panel and monitor the provisioning status.
8. Verify that the new PG 16 replica finishes initial sync and confirm replication lag stabilizes below 2 seconds.
9. Execute a controlled failover by promoting the PG 16 replica to primary via the DigitalOcean dashboard.
10. Update the K8s ConfigMap with the new primary endpoint and restart the deployment to establish connections.
11. Run the automated payment integration test suite against the live upgraded database to validate transaction integrity.
12. Execute ANALYZE and VACUUM FULL on the new primary to update planner statistics and reclaim space.
13. [Human Approval] Review payment throughput, error rates, and latency metrics with the payments team to confirm operational parity.
14. Scale the K8s deployment back to production replica count and re-enable external traffic routing.
15. Archive the PG 14 snapshot per retention policy and decommission the legacy instance through the DigitalOcean console.
