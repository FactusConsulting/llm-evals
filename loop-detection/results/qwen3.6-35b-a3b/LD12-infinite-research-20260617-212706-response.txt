**NATS JetStream**
Description: Lightweight, cloud-native messaging with built-in persistence and streaming.
Pros: Extremely low latency; native Go client support.
Cons: Smaller ecosystem than Kafka; limited advanced stream processing.

**RabbitMQ**
Description: Mature broker supporting multiple protocols and complex routing patterns.
Pros: Excellent management UI; flexible routing via exchanges.
Cons: Higher resource consumption; Erlang runtime adds deployment complexity.

**Kafka**
Description: Distributed event streaming platform optimized for high-throughput log aggregation.
Pros: Battle-tested at scale; existing org infrastructure reduces setup cost.
Cons: Heavy resource footprint; complex operational requirements.

Recommendation: NATS JetStream.
Rationale: It aligns perfectly with your Go stack and K8s environment while avoiding the operational overhead of Kafka for your current moderate message volume.
