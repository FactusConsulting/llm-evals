**NATS JetStream**: Lightweight, cloud-native streaming built on the NATS protocol.
Pros: Extremely low latency and resource footprint; excellent Go client support and K8s operator maturity.
Cons: Smaller ecosystem than competitors; limited advanced stream processing features out-of-the-box.

**RabbitMQ**: Mature, widely adopted broker using the AMQP protocol with flexible routing.
Pros: Rich plugin ecosystem and reliable message delivery guarantees; strong community documentation and tooling.
Cons: Higher memory consumption under load; Erlang runtime adds deployment complexity in constrained K8s nodes.

**Kafka**: Distributed event streaming platform optimized for high-throughput log aggregation.
Pros: Battle-tested durability and horizontal scalability; leverages existing org infrastructure and team familiarity.
Cons: Overly complex for low-volume workloads; heavy operational overhead and resource requirements.

Recommendation: NATS JetStream.
Rationale: It aligns best with your Go/K8s stack, offers the ideal performance-to-resource ratio for your traffic volume, and minimizes operational overhead compared to Kafka or RabbitMQ.
