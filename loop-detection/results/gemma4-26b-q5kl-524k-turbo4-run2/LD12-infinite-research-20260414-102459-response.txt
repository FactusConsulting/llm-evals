1. **Apache Kafka**
A distributed event streaming platform for high-throughput data pipelines.
Pros: Leverages existing organizational expertise, high durability.
Cons: High operational complexity, resource-intensive.

2. **NATS**
A cloud-native, lightweight messaging system for distributed systems.
Pros: Minimal resource footprint, excellent Kubernetes integration.
Cons: Less robust persistence than Kafka, smaller ecosystem.

3. **Redis Streams**
High-performance messaging built on top of Redis data structures.
Pros: Extremely low latency, simple to manage.
Cons: Memory-dependent persistence, requires Redis infrastructure.

**Recommendation: NATS**
NATS provides the most efficient, lightweight solution for your Kubernetes environment while leveraging your team's existing exposure.
