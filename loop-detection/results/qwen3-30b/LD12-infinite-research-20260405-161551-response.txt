NATS Streaming  
Lightweight, cloud-native message queue with strong Kubernetes integration.  
Pros: High performance, easy to scale, integrates well with Go.  
Cons: Limited advanced features, less community support than Kafka.  

RabbitMQ  
Traditional, feature-rich message broker with broad ecosystem.  
Pros: Mature, reliable, strong routing capabilities.  
Cons: Higher resource usage, steeper learning curve.  

Kafka  
Distributed event streaming platform for high-throughput, persistent data.  
Pros: Scalable, durable, great for analytics.  
Cons: Complex setup, heavier on resources.  

RECOMMENDATION: NATS Streaming.
