**Diagnosis:** The service is stuck in a restart loop because it fails to start due to a DNS resolution failure. The application attempts to connect to `kafka.internal:9092`, but the hostname cannot be resolved (`ping: kafka.internal: Name or service not known`). 2. Update the systemd unit to wait for full network readiness by changing `After=network.target` to `After=network-online.target` and adding `Wants=network-online.target` to prevent future startup races.
