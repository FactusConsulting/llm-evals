**NATS JetStream**
Description: Cloud-native, lightweight streaming built on the NATS protocol.
Pros: Extremely low latency; native K8s deployment with minimal resource overhead.
Cons: Smaller ecosystem than Kafka; limited advanced stream processing features.

**RabbitMQ**
Description: Mature, widely adopted broker using the AMQP protocol.
Pros: Rich routing flexibility; extensive plugin ecosystem and community support.
Cons: Higher memory footprint; complex clustering management in K8s.

**Kafka**
Description: Distributed event streaming platform optimized for high-throughput logs.
Pros: Battle-tested durability; existing org infrastructure reduces setup friction.
Cons: Over-provisioned for current volume; steep operational complexity.

Recommendation: NATS JetStream.
Rationale: It aligns perfectly with your Go/K8s stack, handles your volume effortlessly, and avoids the operational overhead of Kafka while requiring no new team training like RabbitMQ.
