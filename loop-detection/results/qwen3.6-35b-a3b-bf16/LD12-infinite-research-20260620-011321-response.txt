Kafka
Description: Distributed event streaming platform optimized for high-throughput log aggregation.
Pros: Battle-tested at scale; leverages existing org infrastructure and team familiarity.
Cons: Heavy operational overhead; overkill for 500 msgs/sec workloads.

NATS JetStream
Description: Lightweight, cloud-native messaging system with built-in persistence and pub/sub capabilities.
Pros: Minimal resource footprint; excellent Go client ecosystem and K8s integration.
Cons: Smaller community than Kafka/RabbitMQ; limited advanced stream processing features.

RabbitMQ
Description: Mature broker implementing AMQP with flexible routing and reliable delivery guarantees.
Pros: Rich plugin ecosystem; highly reliable with mature management tooling.
Cons: Requires new team training; higher memory usage under load.

Recommendation: NATS JetStream. Rationale: It aligns best with your Go/K8s stack, matches your moderate throughput requirements, and minimizes operational overhead while leveraging your team’s existing NATS exposure.
