**Kafka**
Description: Distributed event streaming platform optimized for high-throughput, durable logs.
Pros: Proven scalability; existing org deployment reduces setup overhead.
Cons: Heavy operational footprint; overkill for low-volume workloads.

**NATS JetStream**
Description: Lightweight, cloud-native messaging system with built-in persistence and streaming.
Pros: Minimal resource usage; excellent Go client ecosystem.
Cons: Smaller community; limited advanced routing features.

**RabbitMQ**
Description: Mature broker implementing AMQP with flexible routing and plugin architecture.
Pros: Rich exchange types; strong enterprise support.
Cons: Higher memory consumption; no native Go-first tooling.

Recommendation: NATS JetStream.
Rationale: It aligns perfectly with your Go stack, K8s environment, and moderate traffic while avoiding Kafka’s operational overhead and RabbitMQ’s learning curve.
