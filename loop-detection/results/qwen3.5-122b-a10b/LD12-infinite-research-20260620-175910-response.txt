1. Apache Kafka
Distributed event streaming platform.
Pros: Leverages existing org deployment; Handles massive scale.
Cons: High operational overhead; Resource intensive.

2. NATS JetStream
Cloud-native messaging with persistence.
Pros: Native Go client support; Lightweight K8s footprint.
Cons: Smaller ecosystem; Less mature tooling.

3. RabbitMQ
Advanced message broker with flexible routing.
Pros: Mature protocol support; Easy management UI.
Cons: Team lacks experience; Higher latency.

Recommendation: NATS JetStream.
Rationale: It aligns with Go services and K8s while avoiding Kafka's overhead for low-volume workloads.
