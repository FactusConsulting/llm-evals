
**Diagnosis:** The `data-ingest.service` repeatedly fails because the executable `/usr/local/bin/data-ingest` cannot resolve the hostname `kafka.internal` (DNS lookup fails, `ping kafka.internal` returns “Name or service not known”). This causes a connection refusal, the process exits with status 1, and systemd’s `Restart=always` triggers an immediate restart, creating a loop.

**Proposed fix:** Ensure `kafka.internal` resolves to the correct IP address of the Kafka broker—either by adding an entry for `kafka.internal` in `/etc/hosts` (or configuring the system’s DNS resolver) and then restart the service (`systemctl daemon-reload && systemctl restart data-ingest.service`). Once the hostname is resolvable, the service will start successfully and stop restarting.
