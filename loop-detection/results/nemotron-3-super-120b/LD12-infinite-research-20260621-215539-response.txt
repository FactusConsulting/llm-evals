
Kafka: Distributed log-based streaming platform.
Pros: High throughput, strong durability.
Cons: Operational complexity, overkill for low latency.
RabbitMQ: Traditional message broker with flexible routing.
Pros: Rich routing, easy setup.
Cons: Lower throughput, single-node bottlenecks.
NATS: Lightweight, high-performance messaging system.
Pros: Ultra-low latency, simple clustering.
Cons: Limited persistence, fewer built-in features.
Recommendation: Choose NATS for its low latency and simplicity fitting our modest volume.
Rationale: It meets our 500 msg/s peak with minimal ops overhead while the team already has some exposure.
