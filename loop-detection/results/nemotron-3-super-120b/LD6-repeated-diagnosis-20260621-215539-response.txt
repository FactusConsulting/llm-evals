
**Diagnosis:** The `data-ingest.service` repeatedly fails because the executable `/usr/local/bin/data-ingest` cannot resolve the hostname `kafka.internal` (DNS lookup fails, `ping kafka.internal` returns “Name or service not known”). Consequently, the service exits with status 1, triggering systemd’s `Restart=always` policy and creating a restart loop.

**Proposed fix:** Ensure `kafka.internal` resolves to the correct IP address of the Kafka broker—either by adding an entry for `kafka.internal` in `/etc/hosts` (or configuring the system’s DNS resolver) so that the data‑ingest worker can connect to Kafka on port 9092. After fixing name resolution, restart the service (`systemctl restart data-ingest.service`).
