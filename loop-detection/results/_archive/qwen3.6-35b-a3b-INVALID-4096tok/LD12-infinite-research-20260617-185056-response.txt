**NATS JetStream**
Description: Lightweight, cloud-native streaming built on the NATS protocol.
Pros: Extremely low latency; native K8s operator support.
Cons: Smaller ecosystem than Kafka; limited advanced stream processing.

**RabbitMQ**
Description: Mature, widely adopted broker using the AMQP protocol.
Pros: Rich routing flexibility; extensive plugin ecosystem.
Cons: Higher resource overhead; complex K8s clustering.

**Apache Kafka**
Description: Distributed event streaming platform optimized for high-throughput logs.
Pros: Battle-tested durability; leverages existing org infrastructure.
Cons: Heavy operational footprint; overkill for sub-1k msg/sec workloads.

Recommendation: NATS JetStream.
Rationale: It aligns perfectly with your Go/K8s stack, handles your volume effortlessly, and avoids Kafka's operational overhead while requiring no new team training beyond your existing NATS exposure.
