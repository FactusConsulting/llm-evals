**NATS JetStream**
Description: Lightweight, cloud-native messaging with built-in persistence and streaming.
Pros: Extremely low latency; native Go client with excellent Kubernetes operator support.
Cons: Smaller ecosystem than RabbitMQ; limited advanced routing features.

**RabbitMQ**
Description: Mature, feature-rich broker supporting multiple protocols and complex routing.
Pros: Highly reliable with extensive plugin ecosystem; excellent management UI and tooling.
Cons: Higher resource overhead; Erlang-based architecture complicates custom integrations.

**Kafka**
Description: Distributed event streaming platform optimized for high-throughput log aggregation.
Pros: Battle-tested durability and partitioning; leverages existing org infrastructure.
Cons: Overly complex for low-volume workloads; steep operational learning curve.

Recommendation: NATS JetStream.
Rationale: It aligns perfectly with your Go/Kubernetes stack, handles your moderate throughput efficiently, and avoids the operational overhead of Kafka or RabbitMQ while leveraging your team’s existing NATS exposure.
