**Kafka**
Description: Distributed event streaming platform optimized for high-throughput, durable logs.
Pros: Leverages existing org infrastructure; battle-tested for scale.
Cons: Heavy operational overhead in K8s; overkill for 500 msgs/sec.

**RabbitMQ**
Description: Mature, feature-rich message broker supporting complex routing and reliable delivery.
Pros: Excellent K8s operator support; intuitive AMQP routing.
Cons: Zero team familiarity; higher resource footprint than lightweight alternatives.

**NATS**
Description: Cloud-native, ultra-low-latency messaging system designed for modern distributed architectures.
Pros: Minimal resource usage; native Go client with excellent K8s integration.
Cons: Limited persistent storage out-of-box; smaller ecosystem than Kafka.

Recommendation: NATS JetStream. Rationale: It aligns perfectly with your Go stack, K8s environment, and moderate traffic while avoiding Kafka’s operational burden and RabbitMQ’s learning curve.
