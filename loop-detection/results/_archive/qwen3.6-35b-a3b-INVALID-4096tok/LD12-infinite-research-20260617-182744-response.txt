**NATS JetStream**
Description: Lightweight, cloud-native streaming built on the high-performance NATS protocol.
Pros: Extremely low latency and minimal resource footprint; excellent Go client support and mature K8s operators.
Cons: Smaller ecosystem than Kafka; limited advanced stream processing features out-of-the-box.

**RabbitMQ**
Description: Mature, feature-rich broker specializing in flexible routing and reliable message delivery.
Pros: Highly flexible routing via exchanges; extensive plugin ecosystem and management UI.
Cons: Higher memory consumption under load; Erlang runtime complicates custom integrations.

**Apache Kafka**
Description: Distributed event streaming platform optimized for high-throughput, durable log-based messaging.
Pros: Proven scalability and exactly-once semantics; leverages existing org infrastructure and team familiarity.
Cons: Heavy operational overhead and resource requirements; overkill for moderate message volumes.

I recommend NATS JetStream because its lightweight architecture, native Go support, and low operational overhead align perfectly with your K8s environment and moderate throughput, avoiding Kafka’s complexity while offering better performance than RabbitMQ for your specific stack.
