NATS JetStream: Cloud-native, lightweight streaming built on the NATS protocol.
Pros: Extremely low latency and resource footprint; excellent Go client support and K8s operator maturity.
Cons: Smaller ecosystem than Kafka; limited built-in stream processing tools.

RabbitMQ: Mature, widely adopted message broker using the AMQP protocol.
Pros: Rich routing and plugin ecosystem; battle-tested reliability with strong community support.
Cons: Higher memory/CPU overhead; lacks native streaming semantics for high-throughput event sourcing.

Apache Kafka: Distributed event streaming platform optimized for high-throughput data pipelines.
Pros: Proven scalability and durability; extensive tooling and org-wide familiarity.
Cons: Heavy operational complexity and resource usage; overkill for moderate message volumes.

Recommendation: NATS JetStream.
Rationale: It aligns perfectly with your Go/K8s stack, handles your volume efficiently, and avoids the operational overhead of Kafka while offering better streaming capabilities than RabbitMQ.
