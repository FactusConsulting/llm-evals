**Apache Kafka**
Description: Distributed event streaming platform.
Pros: Existing organizational infrastructure, massive scalability.
Cons: High operational complexity, resource-heavy for low volume.

**NATS**
Description: Cloud-native, high-performance messaging system written in Go.
Pros: Native Go integration, extremely lightweight on Kubernetes.
Cons: Simpler feature set than Kafka, smaller community.

**RabbitMQ**
Description: Feature-rich traditional message broker.
Pros: Advanced routing patterns, wide industry adoption.
Cons: No team familiarity, higher management overhead than NATS.

Recommendation: NATS
Rationale: It aligns perfectly with your Go stack and Kubernetes environment while remaining right-sized for your current message volume.
