**Diagnosis:** The `data-ingest.service" fails because the hostname `kafka.internal" cannot be resolved by the system's DNS, as evidenced by the `ping: kafka.internal: Name or service not known` error. This prevents the service from establishing a connection to the Kafka broker at `kafka.internal:9092`.

**Proposed Fix:** Add a valid DNS record for `kafka.internal` or add an entry mapping `kafka.internal` to the correct IP address in the `/etc/hosts` file to ensure proper hostname resolution.
