**Option 1: Apache Kafka**
A distributed streaming platform designed for high-throughput event logging and stream processing.
Pros: Excellent horizontal scalability; integrates seamlessly with existing analytics infrastructure.
Cons: Operational complexity requires specialized expertise; overkill for low-volume point-to-point tasks.

**Option 2: RabbitMQ**
A mature, open-source message broker implementing the Advanced Message Queuing Protocol (AMQP).
Pros: Highly flexible routing capabilities; easy integration into traditional service architectures.
Cons: Can struggle with extremely high sustained throughput compared to Kafka; scaling complex queues can be challenging.

**Option 3: NATS**
A lightweight, high-performance messaging system optimized for speed and simplicity.
Pros: Extremely low latency and minimal operational overhead; excellent native Kubernetes compatibility.
Cons: Less feature-rich than Kafka for stateful stream processing; lacks built-in persistence guarantees by default.

Recommendation: Given the existing Kafka deployment and the need for reliable, scalable communication within the K8s environment, leveraging Kafka for core services minimizes operational sprawl while maximizing throughput.
