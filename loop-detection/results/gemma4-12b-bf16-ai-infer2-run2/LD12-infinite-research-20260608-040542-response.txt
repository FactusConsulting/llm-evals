**Apache Kafka**
Description: A distributed event streaming platform designed for high throughput and durability.
Pros: High scalability; native integration with existing analytics infrastructure.
Cons: Significant management overhead; excessive complexity for lower message volumes.

**RabbitMQ**
Description: A traditional message broker supporting multiple protocols and sophisticated routing logic.
Pros: Mature feature set; robust reliability guarantees.
Cons: Harder to scale horizontally; lacks built-in stream processing capabilities.

**NATS JetStream**
Description: A lightweight, cloud-native messaging system offering persistence and at-least-once delivery.
Pros: Simplified Kubernetes deployment; minimal resource footprint.
Cons: Smaller community ecosystem; fewer advanced routing features compared to RabbitMQ.

Recommendation: NATS JetStream.
Rationale: It offers the ideal balance of operational simplicity and performance for your specific message volume while leveraging the team's existing experience.
