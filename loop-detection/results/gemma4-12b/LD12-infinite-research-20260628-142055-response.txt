*Description:* A distributed streaming platform designed for high-throughput, persistent log storage.

*Cons:* High operational complexity; overkill for low-volume messaging.

**2. RabbitMQ**
*Description:* A traditional message broker implementing AMQP, MQTT, and STOMP protocols.
*Pros:* Advanced routing capabilities; mature ecosystem.
*Cons:* No prior team experience; vertical scaling limitations.

**3. NATS JetStream**
*Description:* A cloud-native, high-performance messaging system built for modern infrastructure.
*Pros:* Lightweight Kubernetes integration; team familiarity.
*Cons:* Smaller ecosystem than Kafka; less complex routing than RabbitMQ.

NATS JetStream.

**Rationale:**
