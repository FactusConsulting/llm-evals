**Diagnosis:**
The `data-ingest.service` is entering a restart loop because it cannot establish a connection to the Kafka broker at `kafka.internal:9092`. Specifically, the hostname `kafka.internal` is not resolvable on the local system, as evidenced by the `ping` command returning "Name or service not known" and the journal logs reporting a fatal connection failure.

**Proposed Fix:**
Add a corresponding entry for `kafka.internal` to the `/etc/hosts` file mapping it to the correct IP address of the Kafka broker, or update the system's DNS configuration to ensure the hostname resolves correctly.
