**Apache Kafka**
Description: Distributed event streaming platform designed for high-throughput data pipelines.
Pros: High scalability, leverages existing organizational expertise.
Cons: Significant operational complexity, heavy resource consumption.

**NATS**
Description: Lightweight, cloud-native messaging system optimized for modern microservices.
Pros: Minimal footprint, seamless Kubernetes integration.
Cons: Limited message persistence, smaller community ecosystem.

**RabbitMQ**
Description: Mature message broker providing advanced routing capabilities.
Pros: Flexible routing logic, highly reliable.
Cons: Steep learning curve, higher management overhead.

Recommendation: NATS.
Rationale: It offers the lowest operational friction and best alignment with your current Go/Kubernetes stack and peak throughput requirements.
