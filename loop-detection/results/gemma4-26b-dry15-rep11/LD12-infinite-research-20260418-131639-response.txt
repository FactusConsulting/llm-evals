**Apache Kafka**
Description: A distributed event streaming platform built for high-throughput processing.
Pros: Leverages existing organizational infrastructure, proven massive scalability.
Cons: Significant operational complexity, high resource overhead.

**NATS JetStream**
Description: A lightweight, cloud-native solution optimized for high performance.
Pros: Excellent Go service compatibility, minimal resource footprint.
Cons: Smaller developer ecosystem, fewer advanced routing options.

**RabbitMQ**
Description: A versatile message broker specializing in sophisticated routing.
Pros: Rich feature set, mature protocol support.
Cons: Steep learning curve for the team, complex Kubernetes management.

**Recommendation:** NATS JetStream
**Rationale:** Its lightweight architecture and native compatibility with your Go stack provide the most efficient path to deployment without unnecessary complexity.
