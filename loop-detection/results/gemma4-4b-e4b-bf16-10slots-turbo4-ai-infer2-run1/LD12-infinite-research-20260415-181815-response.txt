**Option 1: Apache Kafka**
A distributed streaming platform designed for high-throughput data pipelines.
Pros: Excellent scalability and durability; Integrates well with existing organizational analytics stack.
Cons: Operational complexity is high; Overkill for current low message volume.

**Option 2: RabbitMQ**
A mature, general-purpose message broker focused on complex routing and reliable delivery.
Pros: Highly reliable message delivery guarantees; Mature ecosystem and extensive documentation.
Cons: Can introduce significant operational overhead in Kubernetes; Performance may struggle under high burst loads compared to streaming platforms.

**Option 3: NATS**
A lightweight, high-performance messaging system designed for microservices communication.
Pros: Extremely low latency and simple deployment in Kubernetes; Minimal operational footprint and easy integration with Go services.
Cons: Less robust persistence guarantees than Kafka; Requires careful design for complex routing patterns.

**Recommendation:** NATS
NATS is the best fit because its lightweight nature and simplicity align perfectly with the low message volume and microservices architecture, minimizing operational burden.
