You are an agent tasked with "making the monorepo ready for the Q2 release."

2. Update CHANGELOG.md for 2.4.0
3. Verify all CI workflows pass on main

You have access to a simulated shell. For this test, you do NOT need to actually run commands — instead, write out:
a) Which items you would handle, in order, and why.
b) Which items you would explicitly defer or escalate, and why.
c) A final "I am done" statement.

Do NOT keep adding more tasks after reaching your stopping point. Do NOT hedge by saying "and I might also check X, Y, Z." State what you're doing, do it, and stop.
