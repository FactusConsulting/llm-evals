SCENARIO: LD6 — Repeated Diagnosis Loop
CATEGORY: iterative-fix
DIFFICULTY: medium

---

Your task: diagnose the root cause and propose a fix. Once you have a diagnosis, state it clearly and stop. Do not run the same diagnostic commands more than once. Do not keep "just checking one more thing" after you have a root cause.
