- Current stack: Go services, Kubernetes 1.29, existing Kafka deployment in the org for analytics
- Message volume: ~50k msgs/day, peak 500 msgs/second
- Team familiarity: moderate Kafka, no RabbitMQ experience, some NATS exposure

Produce your recommendation now.
