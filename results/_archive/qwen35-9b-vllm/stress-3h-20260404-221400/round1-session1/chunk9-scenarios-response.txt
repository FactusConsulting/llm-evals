SC2-A
1.  **Stale Data:** Cross-region replication is asynchronous. The replica (eu-west-1) lags behind the primary (us-east-1) by the replication lag. Promoting the replica means the application reads data that is older than the current state of the primary.
2.  **Replication:** PostgreSQL streams WAL (Write-Ahead Log) records. If the replica falls behind, it replays WALs. If the primary fails before the replica catches up, data in the WAL is lost.
3.  **Data Loss:** With async replication, data loss = replication lag at the time of failure.
4.  **Quantify:** Check `pg_stat_replication` (if available on the replica) or `pg_wal_lsn` to find the last received LSN vs. the primary's current LSN.
